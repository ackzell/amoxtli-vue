# Directives

In Vue, directives are special <AvTooltipTrigger id="some-id">tokens</AvTooltipTrigger> in the markup that tell the library to do something to a DOM element. They are prefixed with `v-` to indicate that they are Vue-specific attributes. Directives can be used to bind data to elements, conditionally render elements, loop through data, and more.
